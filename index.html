Common questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Do you offer emergency callouts?

Yes — we understand electrical problems can happen anytime. We provide emergency repairs across Sydney. Call us directly on 0432 740 124.

What type of properties do you work on?

We handle everything from homes and apartments through to shops, offices, warehouses and industrial sites. We also work with builders on granny flats, new builds and multi-unit developments.

Do I need a licensed electrician in NSW?

Yes — by law in NSW, all electrical work must be carried out by a licensed electrician. DIY electrical work is dangerous and may void your home insurance. Our team is fully licensed (Lic. 401509C) and insured.

How do I book a job?

Simply fill out our free quote form above or call us directly on 0432 740 124. We'll get back to you within 24 hours to confirm details and lock in a time.

How quickly can you get to me?

For emergencies we aim to respond as fast as possible — often same day. For standard bookings we're typically available within a few business days depending on current workload.

Do you provide a certificate of compliance?

Yes — all completed electrical work comes with a Certificate of Compliance as required by NSW Fair Trading. This protects you for insurance purposes and confirms the work meets Australian standards.

How JNM Electrical Services collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 22 June 2026

Overview

JNM Electrical Services (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we look after it when you contact us, request a quote, or use our website.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, request a quote, or fill out a form on our website or through our Google Ads lead forms, we may collect:

Your name

Phone number

Email address

Property address (where relevant to a quote)

Details about the job or service you're enquiring about

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

Respond to your enquiry or quote request

Schedule and carry out electrical work you've requested

Send you a quote, invoice, or follow-up about your job

Improve our services and customer communication

We do not use your information for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Sharing Your Information

We don't sell your personal information. We only share it with trusted third parties where necessary to run our business, such as:

Booking, scheduling, or job management software we use

Accounting or invoicing platforms

Google Ads, where you've submitted a lead form (Google's own privacy policy also applies)

We don't share your details with any other party for their own marketing purposes.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your information for as long as needed to complete your job, manage warranty or follow-up work, and meet our legal and tax record-keeping obligations. After that, we securely delete or de-identify it.

Your Rights

You can ask us to:

Tell you what personal information we hold about you

Correct any information that's inaccurate or out of date

Delete your information, where we're not required to keep it for legal reasons

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, loss, or unauthorised access, including secure storage of digital records and limited staff access.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
