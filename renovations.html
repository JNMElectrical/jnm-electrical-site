Renovation electrical questions answered.

Yes — any change to fixed wiring in NSW must be done by a licensed electrician. This includes adding a rangehood circuit, moving a stove outlet, adding power points, or upgrading lighting. Doing it yourself or using an unlicensed tradie is illegal and will void your insurance.
Absolutely. Jacob regularly works as the electrical sub-contractor on renovation projects. He'll coordinate directly with your builder around the schedule — first fix, second fix, final inspections — to keep things moving.
First fix is the rough-in work done before walls are closed — running cables, conduit, and in-wall components. Second fix happens after plastering and tiling — fitting off switches, power points, lights, and appliances. JNM handles both stages.
Not always, but often worth checking. Older homes may have ceramic fuses that need to be upgraded to safety switches before extra circuits can be added. Jacob will assess your existing switchboard during the quote and advise if an upgrade is needed.
Yes — every job gets a Certificate of Compliance (CoC) issued. This is a legal requirement in NSW and is important for your home insurance and future property sale. JNM always issues these as standard, not an extra.
